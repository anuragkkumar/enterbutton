# ⚡ Laptop Remote

> Control your Windows laptop from your phone over local Wi-Fi. Fast, private, and zero setup.

![Windows](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Windows%2010%20%2F%2011-0078d4)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-green)

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Start**: Double-click **`start.bat`** on your laptop.
2. **Connect**: Scan the terminal QR code with your phone camera (must be on the same Wi-Fi).
3. **Control**: That's it! Opens instantly in your phone's browser with 1ms latency.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔘 **Enter**: Giant glowing Enter button + quick chips (`Y + Enter`, `N + Enter`, `Lock Screen`).
- 💬 **Writer**: Type or use phone voice dictation to type directly into your laptop cursor.
- 🖱️ **Trackpad**: Smooth 60 FPS cursor control, tap-to-click, and two-finger scroll.
- ⚡ **Ultra-Fast**: Direct local LAN connection with near-zero latency (~1–3ms).
- 🔒 **100% Private**: Runs strictly on your home Wi-Fi — zero external internet exposure.

---

## 🛑 How to Stop
Double-click **`stop.bat`** or close the terminal window.
